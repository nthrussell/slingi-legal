Report an Issue

To help us investigate, please include:

  • A description of the problem and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps that led to the problem.
  • Your device model, operating system version, and Slingi app version.
  • A screenshot or the error message, if available.

Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information. Hide any private information in screenshots before sending them.
